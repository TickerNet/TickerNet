# Name and logo

The TickerNet name and logo identify the open-source project in this repository. These rules keep the name
meaning one thing: the open-source TickerNet, released under the pledge in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## You may

- Say that your product uses, is built on, or is compatible with TickerNet.
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials and courses about TickerNet.
- Use the name for an unchanged copy of a TickerNet release.
- Name packages that extend TickerNet `<YourName>.TickerNet.<Something>`, as long as the description makes clear
  they are not part of the TickerNet project.

## You may not

- Publish a fork or modified version under the TickerNet name or logo. Give it its own name; you may say it is
  "based on TickerNet".
- Publish a proprietary, source-available or paid product under the TickerNet name.
- Publish packages whose IDs start with `TickerNet.` unless they come from this project.
- Suggest that the TickerNet project or Omniscient AI endorses your product when it doesn't.

## Omniscient AI's name and logos

The Omniscient AI logos in `site/assets/sponsor/` credit TickerNet's sponsor. They are trademarks of Omniscient AI
Private Limited and are not covered by the MIT or Apache-2.0 license. Remove them from forks and derivatives, and
don't use them to suggest that Omniscient AI endorses your product.

## TickerQ

TickerNet is a fork of TickerQ by Arcenox LLC. TickerQ is Arcenox LLC's name. We use it only to describe where
TickerNet came from and how to move to it from TickerQ. We do not use their logo.
