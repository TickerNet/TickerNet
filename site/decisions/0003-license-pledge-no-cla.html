ADR 0003 · Origin and license

License MIT OR Apache-2.0 with an open-source pledge and no CLA

TickerNet keeps TickerQ's dual license, adds an irrevocable pledge that every release stays open source, and takes contributions under a DCO sign-off instead of a contributor license agreement.

Status
Accepted
Decided
2026-11-23
Affects
LICENSE, GOVERNANCE.md, TRADEMARKS.md, CONTRIBUTING.md

Related: ADR 0001 Fork TickerQ at its last open-source commit, ADR 0004 Keep a clean room around TickerQ's commercial versions, ADR 0007 Omniscient AI sponsors TickerNet, which lives in its own GitHub organization

01

Context

TickerQ moved from MIT OR Apache-2.0 to a commercial license (ADR 0001). Its CLA.md, at the fork point, granted Arcenox LLC the right to use contributions "under any license, including proprietary licenses" (section 2). An agreement like that lets one party relicense everyone's work. People who adopt TickerNet need to know it can't happen again.

02

Decision

  • Keep the dual license, MIT OR Apache-2.0, from upstream: LICENSE, and PackageLicenseExpression in src/Directory.Build.props.
  • GOVERNANCE.md makes an irrevocable pledge: every release stays open source; no commercial edition under the TickerNet name; no license changes by assignment; and a handover keeps the pledge.
  • No CLA. Contributors keep their copyright and sign off each commit under the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 (CONTRIBUTING.md, rule 1). .github/workflows/dco.yaml checks every pull request.
  • TRADEMARKS.md reserves the TickerNet name for the open-source project.
03

Alternatives considered

  • Copyleft, such as MPL-2.0 or LGPL. Rejected: it would limit proprietary derivatives, which the project wants to allow.
  • MIT only. Rejected: it loses Apache-2.0's patent grant.
  • A CLA. Rejected: it gives one party the rights to relicense everyone's work, which is the mechanism that let TickerQ relicense.
04

Consequences

  • The pledge binds the project and its name, not its users. Anyone can build proprietary products on TickerNet, but not under the TickerNet name.
  • No single party collects the rights to relicense contributions.
  • A later step could move the name and package IDs to an open-source foundation. Pledge item 4 already requires that, or a transfer to the active maintainers, if the steward stops supporting the project.
  • GOVERNANCE.md changes only through a pull request with an ADR, and the pledge may never be weakened.
